FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our WordPress development service.

How long does it take to build a WordPress website?

Most business websites are delivered within 5–7 working days after we receive your content (text and images). Complex websites may take slightly longer depending on requirements.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Yes, you provide your business text and images. If you don't have content ready, we can guide you on what's needed, or provide basic content as an add-on service.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Absolutely! We build websites using Elementor and standard WordPress, which is very user-friendly. We also provide basic training so you can update content easily.

Does the price include domain and hosting?

The starting price covers the website design and development. Domain and hosting are separate and typically cost around PKR 3,000–5,000/year. We can help you set these up.

Do you provide support after the website is live?

Yes! Every project includes 30 days of free support for minor changes and bug fixes. For ongoing maintenance, we offer affordable monthly support packages.
